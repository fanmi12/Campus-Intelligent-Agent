全球交流合作处 介绍

全球交流合作处是直属东莞城市学院的二级行政单位，主要负责学校与境内外高校及学术组织的交流合作，组织管理师生对外交流项目，学校专业的国际认证，国际与港澳台学生招生以及境外教师管理等工作；目前，学校已与30多所高校建立良好合作关系，深入开展各类交流项目，其中包括：学期交流、3+1本科双学位、硕士研究生保荐等合作项目。通过开展一系列境外交流学习项目，为我校广大师生提供丰富的学习机会，师生们可体验已对接优质课程与教学质量，深入感受跨文化交流，拓宽全球视野。

2017年9月，城市学院建立了第一个海外实习实践基地，重在对学生国际视野培养，增进跨文化、多文化交流与包容。目前已与中国对外服务友好中心、斯里兰卡等机构和国家建立起合作关系。

Global Exchange and Cooperation Office Global Exchange and Cooperation Office is the secondary unit of CityU DG. It is mainly responsible for the exchanges and cooperation between the college and overseas universities or academic organizations, the organization of students, teachers exchange and study, and the management of foreign teachers.At present, the school has extensive exchanges and cooperation with more than 30 well-known universities such as Midwestern State University, University of the West of England,University of Canberra, and the University of Southern Queensland, including short-term exchange project and the “2+2”, “3+1” dual degree programs, further master study of international perspective. It has carried out Postgraduate Program with the Hong Kong Institute of Education and Lingnan University, postgraduate recommendation programs with Macau City University. In addition, the International Exchange Center has a language training center, and offers IELTS intensive courses and Korean language courses to create a good international deep learning atmosphere.In September 2017, CityU DG has found the first overseas internship base, focusing on cultivating students' international vision and enhancing intercultural and multi-cultural communication and inclusion. Now it has established a cooperative relationship with China's Foreign Friendship Service Center, Sri Lanka and other institutions and countries, including programs like paid internship in the United States and international volunteer internship for non-profit organizations in Sri Lanka.（城院国际交流中心官方公众号）
